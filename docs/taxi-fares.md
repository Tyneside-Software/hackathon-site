# Taxi fares

Worked check of the North Tyneside hackney carriage tariff that took effect on Monday 11 November 2024. The brief is [Tracker, office, and game](#taxi). This page checks that brief's one-mile and three-mile examples against the council page, and adds the waiting-time cases. It does not replace the brief's table.

Source: [Taxi ranks and tariffs](https://www.northtyneside.gov.uk/licensing/taxis-and-private-hire/taxi-ranks-and-tariffs), read on 24 October 2026.

The August 2026 proposal is not on that page. This worksheet does not use it. The November 2024 prices below are unchanged.

## What the council page says

**Tariff one.** Other times. From Monday 11 November 2024.

| Step | Charge |
|------|--------|
| First 161.3 yards or 46.8 seconds, or a part of that | £2.40 |
| Each further 161.3 yards or 46.8 seconds, or a part of that | £0.25 |

**Tariff two.** From the same date. Hirings between 10pm and 7am, all day on Sundays, bank holidays, and 27, 28, 29, 30 and 31 December.

| Step | Charge |
|------|--------|
| First 140.5 yards or 45 seconds, or a part of that | £3.15 |
| Each further 140.5 yards or 45 seconds, or a part of that | £0.25 |

The brief's 22:00–07:00 is the same window as the council's 10pm to 7am. The yardage, the seconds, and both prices match the brief.

A unit ends when the distance or the waiting time runs out, whichever is first. Charge that many units. Do not add a distance meter to a time meter.

The distance and waiting cases below add no extras. Extras are a separate amount on top of the meter. This page does not change the game money lines in the brief.

## Distance check

No waiting and no extras. One mile is 1760 yards. Three miles is 5280 yards. A part of a further unit is a whole further unit.

| Journey | Tariff | First unit | Left | Further units | Fare |
|---------|--------|------------|------|---------------|------|
| 1 mile | one | £2.40 | 1598.7 yards | 10 | £4.90 |
| 3 miles | one | £2.40 | 5118.7 yards | 32 | £10.40 |
| 1 mile | two | £3.15 | 1619.5 yards | 12 | £6.15 |
| 3 miles | two | £3.15 | 5139.5 yards | 37 | £12.40 |

Tariff one, 3 miles: 5280 − 161.3 = 5118.7 yards. 5118.7 / 161.3 is 31 units and 118.4 yards over, so 32 further units. £2.40 + 32 × £0.25 = £2.40 + £8.00 = £10.40.

Tariff two, 3 miles: 5280 − 140.5 = 5139.5 yards. 5139.5 / 140.5 is 36 units and 81.5 yards over, so 37 further units. £3.15 + 37 × £0.25 = £3.15 + £9.25 = £12.40.

Tariff one, 1 mile: 1760 − 161.3 = 1598.7 yards. 1598.7 / 161.3 is 9 units and 147 yards over, so 10 further units. £2.40 + £2.50 = £4.90.

Tariff two, 1 mile: 1760 − 140.5 = 1619.5 yards. 1619.5 / 140.5 is 11 units and 74 yards over, so 12 further units. £3.15 + £3.00 = £6.15.

These four fares are the brief's worked examples. They use only the council page's November 2024 unit sizes and prices.

## Waiting time

The car is hired and does not move, so only the clock completes a unit. The same number of units as a distance example is the same fare.

| Wait | Tariff | Units | Fare |
|------|--------|-------|------|
| 514.8 seconds (8 minutes 34.8 seconds), 11 × 46.8 | one | 11 | £4.90 |
| 1544.4 seconds (25 minutes 44.4 seconds), 33 × 46.8 | one | 33 | £10.40 |
| 585 seconds (9 minutes 45 seconds), 13 × 45 | two | 13 | £6.15 |
| 1710 seconds (28 minutes 30 seconds), 38 × 45 | two | 38 | £12.40 |

Tariff one, 514.8 seconds: the first 46.8 seconds is £2.40. Ten further units are £2.50. Total £4.90, the same as one mile.

Tariff one, 1544.4 seconds: the first unit is £2.40 and 32 further units are £8.00. Total £10.40, the same as three miles.

Tariff two, 585 seconds: the first unit is £3.15 and 12 further units are £3.00. Total £6.15, the same as one mile.

Tariff two, 1710 seconds: the first unit is £3.15 and 37 further units are £9.25. Total £12.40, the same as three miles.

A part of a further unit still counts.

| Wait | Tariff | Units | Fare |
|------|--------|-------|------|
| 514.9 seconds, 0.1 seconds over 11 × 46.8 | one | 12 | £5.15 |
| 585.1 seconds, 0.1 seconds over 13 × 45 | two | 14 | £6.40 |

Tariff one: £2.40 + 11 × £0.25 = £5.15. Tariff two: £3.15 + 13 × £0.25 = £6.40.

## Mixed, not added

Yards and seconds are not two bills. Each unit is one or the other.

**Tariff one.** The car covers the first 161.3 yards in 20 seconds. Distance finishes that unit before 46.8 seconds, so it is the £2.40 flag. The car then waits, not moving, for 46.8 seconds. Time finishes the next unit. Fare £2.65.

The journey used 161.3 yards and 66.8 seconds. A distance meter on its own is 1 unit. A time meter on its own is 2 units. Adding them makes 3 units and £2.90. The tariff charges 2 units and £2.65.

**Tariff two.** The car covers the first 140.5 yards in 20 seconds. That is the £3.15 flag. It then waits 45 seconds, which is one further unit at £0.25. Fare £3.40.

That journey is 140.5 yards and 65 seconds. Adding a 1-unit distance meter to a 2-unit time meter makes 3 units and £3.65. The tariff charges 2 units and £3.40.

## Extras

November 2024 extras are added to the meter. They do not replace it. A guide or hearing dog is exempt. Tolls are passed on at cost and have no fixed figure on this page.

| Extra | Pence |
|-------|------:|
| Soiling the carriage | 12500 |
| Each passenger after two | 50 |
| Each bag carried outside | 40 |
| Each animal, except a guide or hearing dog | 300 |
| A guide or hearing dog | 0 |
| Each bicycle | 300 |
| Each pram | 50 |

Worked quote. Tariff one, 3 miles, no waiting, one extra passenger, one bag carried outside. The meter is the three-mile tariff one fare, 1040 pence (£10.40). The extras are 50 + 40 = 90 pence. The total is 1130 pence (£11.30). `scripts/taxi_fare_check.py` requires 1130.
