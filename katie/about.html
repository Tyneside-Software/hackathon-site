About this shop

Katie’s fidget squish shop, built on the Tyneside hackathon site. Same idea as the Logistics progress page: the steps, in order, so nobody has to guess what already landed.

The steps How it is wired Live

The steps

  1. 01
    Put the shop on the live hackathon site. Katie’s pages were copied onto Tyneside-Software/hackathon-site so they serve at hackathon.tyneside.software/katie. A root katie.html sends /katie to the folder.
  2. 02
    Homemade items show in stock or out of stock. Each homemade card got a stock label so you can see what is for sale.
  3. 03
    Password Admin. A locked Admin page lets you change names, prices, photos, stock, and add or remove items. Password cassiethecat. Admin is its own menu item; leaving it logs you out.
  4. 04
    Pencil to pick pictures. On a phone the pencil opens the gallery. On a computer it opens picture files. You can add more than one photo per item.
  5. 05
    Blue, baby blue, purple and pink background. The old white page became a swirl gradient. Light-mode panels are pink #f0c0e5. Rainbow bits are pastel in light mode and bright in dark mode.
  6. 06
    Dark mode toggle. Top-left. Light mode: pastel rainbow, says Dark mode. Dark mode: bright rainbow, says Light mode.
  7. 07
    Stock counts and a star burst. Admin sets how many of an item are in stock. Cards show the number. In-stock photos get an animated star burst. Squishies show stock labels too.
  8. 08
    Reviews. Rate an item out of 5, write a description, optionally add a photo. Cards show the average, including half stars.
  9. 09
    Product pages, like a mini Amazon. Tap a card for the full item: all pictures, share, reviews, and suggested products underneath. Item names are #72cefc. The title next to the heart is the same pinks as the heart.
  10. 10
    Mystery and Fidgets. Two extra categories in the menu, with their own pages. Add items to them in Admin.
  11. 11
    Built by Lewis, plus the legal bit. The top-right note says built by lewis. Scroll to the bottom for terms and conditions and lewis was here.
  12. 12
    This About page. A running list of how the shop was made, in the shop’s own theme. New work gets a new step.

How it is wired

  1. 01
    Static files on GitHub Pages. HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript in the katie folder. Push main and it goes live at hackathon.tyneside.software/katie.
  2. 02
    Admin and reviews save on this computer. Catalog edits and reviews live in the browser. Download stock.json from Admin if you want the live shop for everyone to match.
  3. 03
    Buy is email. Email to buy goes to katie@tyneside.software. There is no checkout cart.
