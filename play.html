            How to play

            The idea

            You run a taxi firm on Tyneside. The clock is real time: one second here is one second in the game. You start with ten taxis at your own depot. Shiremoor Taxis starts at Shiremoor. North Taxis and South Taxis start at their own depots. People appear around the map through the day. The closest free taxi, across the three firms, collects them.

            Michael, Noah, and Reeve share one map. Each firm can see the other two. They are rivals and compete for the same fares. The closest free taxi wins. Rename the company from the session panel. The aim is to keep your cars on paid work, beat the rivals to waiting people, and grow the fleet.

            Jobs

            Demand follows the time of day: morning commute into town, daytime coast and hospitals, evening reverse, nights out. A waiting person is claimed by the nearest free taxi. That taxi drives to them (collecting), then to the drop (carrying), then is free again.

            • Click a taxi on the map or in Your fleet to follow it. The map zooms once to that taxi and its route. After that you can pan and zoom freely; the route stays on the map. The dotted moving line is where it is going. The solid line is where it has already been. Only one taxi’s route is shown at a time.
            • Stop following zooms out just far enough to see every taxi.
            • Cancel this job drops a fare you do not want. If the car has already left, miles driven still cost fuel and there is no fare.

            Loiter

            A loiter patch is a circle you place on the map. Idle taxis of that firm wait inside it instead of hunting around or sitting at the depot. Cars already on a job keep that job. Two idle taxis will not sit on the same point. An empty drive to the patch is at most two miles.

            Press Draw area, then click and drag on the map to draw the circle (0.2 to 8 km). Firm is blank for your cars, or north / south to park that rival. The blue circle on the map is the patch.

            Use it to park spare cars near busy places (town in the morning, the coast in the afternoon) so the next person is closer than the depot.

            Money

            The panel on the right is your ledger, in pounds:

            • fare in — what passengers paid on finished trips (North Tyneside tariff)
            • driver share — half of fare in
            • fuel — £0.20 a mile, including empty running
            • standing — £15.00 per taxi you own
            • cash — what you can spend

            A new taxi costs £8,000.00. Click the taxi marked for sale, then buy it from that panel. It waits a few seconds at the depot, then takes a fare.
