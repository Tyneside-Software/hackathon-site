How a card ships

  2. Home, Map, and Board still load if the new thing is half-finished — fallbacks, never a blank page.
  3. Move the card to Ready to demo when someone else can click it without a briefing.
  4. No Cloud Run until card 10. LocalStorage is the persistence story until then.
